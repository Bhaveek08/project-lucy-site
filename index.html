Which model?

So it's ChatGPT with a face?
No — nothing leaves the device. Her own memory, her own moods, and she acts without being asked.
What happens when it gets something wrong?
It's caught by a test, or by her checking her own output — that's most of what I've built.

A stranger on Discord can make her say something. They can't reach the machine — that path never touches device control.

Companionship first. But she earns — videos and paid website work — and she runs the machines.
