Privacy Policy

L.U.C.Y — Discord application & website
Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version

  • Lucy runs on a private machine, not a cloud service. Her memory lives there.
  • In a server she only reads messages that mention her. She is not logging your server.
  • In a DM she reads what you send her, because that's what a DM is.
  • Some messages are sent to Google's Gemini API when she wants a second opinion, and search queries go to search engines when she looks something up. Details below.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for advertising. There is no advertising.
  • Say "stop" and she blocks you permanently. Email us and your data is deleted.

1. Who is responsible

Lucy is built and operated by a private individual in India — not a company. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 that person is the Data Fiduciary; for the UK/EU GDPR, the Data Controller. Contact: lucentunboundconciousyield@gmail.com.

2. What she collects

DataWhenWhy
Your Discord user ID and username Whenever you interact with her To tell people apart, to remember a conversation, and to honour a block
Message content you send her In a DM, or in a server channel when you mention or name her To understand and answer you
Channel and server ID, timestamp Same To reply in the right place, and to apply rate limits
What she sent you Every message she sends An audit log, so her operator can see exactly what she said to whom
Block records When you tell her to stop To make the block permanent. This record is kept even after deletion requests — otherwise the block would be forgotten and she'd message you again

What she does not collect

  • Messages in servers that don't mention her. She isn't reading your channels.
  • Your email, phone number, address or payment details. She has no payments.
  • Voice or video from Discord. The Discord app is text only.
  • Your IP address. Discord handles the connection; she never sees it.
  • Anything from your camera or microphone. Those exist in her private local build and are never connected to Discord.

Please don't send her secrets. Passwords, card numbers, government IDs, medical records, or other people's personal information. She has no way to un-see them, and they'd end up in the log described above.

3. Where your data goes

This is the part most policies are vague about, so here it is exactly.

3.1 Stored locally

Her memory, conversation history and logs are stored on the operator's own hardware in India. They are not on a hosted database and not on anyone else's server.

3.2 Third parties that may receive your message content

WhoWhat they getWhen
Discord Inc. Everything, inevitably They operate the platform. Their privacy policy applies
Google (Gemini API) The text of your question, or part of it When she consults a second opinion to check her own reasoning. Not every message — but assume it can happen to any message
Search engines A search query derived from your question When she looks something up rather than answering from memory
Google Fonts Your IP and browser, on this website only When you load a page here. Not connected to the Discord app

Nobody else. No analytics, no advertising networks, no data brokers, no tracking pixels, no selling. There is nothing to sell and no intention to.

The language model that actually forms her replies runs locally. Google is a consultant she sometimes asks, not the thing she is.

4. Legal basis and purpose

Under GDPR, processing rests on legitimate interests (operating a conversational service you chose to talk to) and, for optional memory features, consent. Under the DPDP Act, on your consent given by choosing to interact with her. You can withdraw at any time — see section 6.

Your data is used to answer you, to remember conversations, to enforce blocks and rate limits, and to fix bugs. It is not used to train or fine-tune models. Lucy's training data comes from her operator's own material and public datasets, not from users' messages.

6. Your rights, and how to actually use them

You have the right to know what's held about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to have it deleted, to withdraw consent, and to object to processing. Under GDPR you may also complain to your local supervisory authority; under the DPDP Act, to the Data Protection Board of India.

The fast ways

  • Stop her messaging you: say stop, leave me alone, or don't message me in a DM. It takes effect immediately and permanently. No confirmation, no attempt to talk you out of it.
  • Make her forget something: ask her directly — "forget what I told you about X".
  • Delete everything: email lucentunboundconciousyield@gmail.com from any address, with your Discord username and user ID. Answered within 30 days. Everything goes except the block record, for the reason given above.
  • Get a copy: same address, same 30 days.

Requests are free. We may ask you to confirm the request from your Discord account, so that somebody else can't delete your data or read it by claiming to be you.

7. Children

Lucy is not for anyone under 13, or under the higher minimum age Discord sets in your country. She does not knowingly collect data from children. Under the DPDP Act, processing a child's data requires verifiable parental consent, which this service is not set up to obtain — so it does not process children's data at all. If you believe a child's data is held, email lucentunboundconciousyield@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

8. Security

Data sits on personal hardware with full-disk encryption and no public inbound access. API keys and tokens are kept in local environment files that are never committed to source control and never logged. Access is limited to the operator.

Honestly: this is a personal project, not a bank. It is defended seriously but it is one person's machine. Please calibrate what you share accordingly.

If a breach affects your rights, affected users will be notified and the Data Protection Board of India informed, as the DPDP Act requires. Found a hole? Tell us at lucentunboundconciousyield@gmail.com — you'll get thanks, not lawyers.

9. International transfers

Data is held in India. If you're in the UK/EU, using the service means your data is processed in India, and by Google and Discord in the United States and elsewhere. Those transfers rely on your consent and on the third parties' own transfer mechanisms.

10. Automated decisions

Lucy makes no decision that produces a legal or similarly significant effect on you. She talks. The one automated action she takes about you is blocking, which you trigger yourself and which only ever reduces contact.

11. This website

project-lucy.me is a static site on GitHub Pages. No cookies are set, no analytics run, and no account is needed. GitHub records standard server logs; Google Fonts sees your IP when fonts load. That is the entire extent of it.

12. Grievance Officer

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires a Data Fiduciary to publish a contact for grievances. For this service that is the operator, reachable at lucentunboundconciousyield@gmail.com.

Put GRIEVANCE at the start of the subject line. Complaints are acknowledged within 7 days and resolved within 30. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the UK or EU, you may instead complain to your local supervisory authority.

13. Your duties as a user

Under the DPDP Act, a Data Principal also has duties. Concretely, for this service: do not impersonate someone else when making a data request, do not file a false or frivolous grievance, and do not submit other people's personal data to Lucy without their knowledge. Requests that appear to be an attempt to obtain another person's data will be refused.

14. If something goes wrong

If a personal-data breach occurs, affected users will be notified without undue delay through Discord and, where an email address is known, by email. The Data Protection Board of India will be informed as the DPDP Act requires, and any applicable GDPR notification will be made within 72 hours of becoming aware.

Notification will state what happened, what data was involved, what has been done about it, and what you should do.

15. Changes

This policy may change. The version and date at the top change with it. If a change materially affects how your data is used, it will be announced through the Service where reasonably possible.
