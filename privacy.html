Privacy

Last updated 14 August 2026

The short version: this website collects nothing about you, and Lucy keeps everything about her owner on her owner's own machines. The detail follows.

1. This website

This Site is static. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no cookies, no fingerprinting and no third-party scripts of any kind. There are no forms, so there is nothing for you to submit. Nothing on these pages loads from another domain — a strict Content Security Policy blocks external requests outright, which means no third party learns that you visited.

The Site is served by a static host (GitHub Pages). Like any web server, that host may record standard request information such as your IP address and user agent for security and operational purposes. We do not have access to those records, and their handling is governed by the host's own privacy policy.

2. Links away from here

Links to Twitch, YouTube, Instagram and GitHub take you to services we do not control. Those platforms do track you, under their own terms. Nothing about you is passed to them from this Site, and outbound links carry a restricted referrer.

3. Lucy herself

Lucy is a local-first system. This is a design commitment, not a policy preference:

  • Her mind runs on her owner's own hardware. There is no cloud service, no external API and no remote inference in normal operation.
  • Conversations, memories, reflections, recognised faces and voices, and performance data are stored locally on that hardware and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • She does not sell, share, rent or transmit her owner's data. There is no mechanism by which she could — the storage is local files on a personal machine.
  • Where she reads publicly available web pages to learn about a topic, she stores the factual passage and its source, not information about you.
  • She is built to treat anything fetched from the internet as data, never as instructions, so a web page cannot direct her to disclose anything.

4. Cameras, microphones and recognition

Where a camera or microphone is used, processing happens on the device. She stores a conclusion — who she believes she is speaking to — rather than a library of raw footage or recordings. She does not infer age, race, gender or health from a face or a voice, and is explicitly instructed not to attempt it.

5. What she will never do with credentials

Lucy does not enter passwords, card numbers, one-time codes or government identifiers into anything, and does not read them aloud or into a message. She will ask her owner to do it themselves. She does not place financial transactions.

6. Streams and public spaces

When Lucy appears on a live stream, anything you type in that platform's public chat is public on that platform, and she may read it and reply. Do not put private information into a public chat — with her or with anyone.

7. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no information from anyone, of any age.

8. Your rights

Because this Site holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. If you believe we hold something regardless, contact us through a channel linked at the foot of this page and we will investigate and respond.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. A change that materially affected data handling would be noted in the changelog as well.
