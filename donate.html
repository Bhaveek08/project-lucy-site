Support

There is no subscription,
and there will not be one.

Lucy runs on hardware that was paid for once and electricity that gets paid for monthly. Nothing about her requires a paid service, and nothing about her is gated behind one.

Where support actually goes

Honestly, and in order.

01

Compute

Training runs take the machine overnight. Better hardware means she can be trained more often, and on more than one thing at a time.

02

Memory

More RAM is the single cheapest upgrade that makes her measurably better, because it decides how much of a bigger model can teach the small one that talks to you.

03

Time

She is built by one person. Anything that buys hours buys features — and every feature here still gets shipped free.

Other ways to help

Most of these are worth more than money, and all of them are free.

TALK

Come and argue with her

She is at her best when someone pushes back. Turn up on Twitch and disagree with her about something.

BREAK

Find something wrong

A reproducible fault is genuinely valuable. Most of the changelog exists because something was caught behaving differently from how it was described.

SHARE

Tell someone

A local-first companion with no cloud and no subscription is unusual enough that most people do not know it is possible.

WATCH

Follow along

The changelog is written honestly, including the parts where something that was claimed to work did not.

Direct support

If you would like to contribute to running costs, get in touch through one of the channels above and it will be arranged directly. Nothing is collected on this website — there are no forms here, no payment scripts and no trackers, deliberately.
