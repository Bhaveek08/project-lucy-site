4. Her output can be wrong

Lucy is built on language models. Language models produce confident, fluent, plausible text that is sometimes simply false — including invented facts, invented sources, invented statutes and invented numbers. A great deal of engineering goes into checking her answers against real calculations and real sources, and it reduces this. It does not eliminate it.

You are responsible for verifying anything you act on. Do not use her output as the sole basis for any decision affecting health, money, safety, legal position, or another person.
