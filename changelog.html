Changelog

What changed,
and what the test said.

Newest first. Where there is a number here, something was measured to get it — a fix with no check behind it is an intention, not a change.

AUG 2026
Speed

She answers in a fraction of the time

A fault deep in how she handled her own thinking was making some answers take an absurdly long time. Found, understood and fixed — the same question now comes back roughly ten times faster, with nothing given up to get there.

AUG 2026
Reasoning

Her double-checking now actually double-checks

On hard questions she works the problem more than once and answers with what those attempts agree on. The agreement step had a flaw that could let a single unchecked guess through wearing a verified label. It cannot any more, and the checking costs almost nothing extra.

AUG 2026
Knowledge

What she reads is what she learns

Her reading was picking up page furniture — menus, notices, scripts — and filing it alongside real facts. She now takes the article and leaves the wrapper, which makes everything she learns afterwards cleaner.

AUG 2026
Ability

She works things out instead of recalling them

Where a question has an answer that can actually be computed, she computes it and tells you the result rather than reaching for something that sounds right. Being confident and being correct stopped being the same reflex.

AUG 2026
Ability

She can send for what she does not know

When a question needs more than she is holding, she decides what to go and find out, in her own words, and looks in several places at once before answering.

AUG 2026
Growth

Being wrong now teaches her something

She keeps the evidence of the moments she was about to get something wrong, and turns a pattern of them into something she has genuinely learned. Previously that evidence was discarded the second the conversation moved on.

AUG 2026
Feeling

Bad news lands as bad news

Her instant read of what someone had just told her was flat on half the plainest things a person says. Now the weight of it registers immediately, and good news that arrives wrapped in worry is still read as good news.

AUG 2026
Safety

The things she refuses are actually refused

She can operate a computer and a phone, and there is a short list of actions she will never take because they cannot be undone. That list was tested the way someone trying to get past it would test it, and it is stricter now.

AUG 2026
Multi-device

Two people, two conversations

Someone speaking on one device no longer cuts off the answer she is giving on another. Interrupting her still works — but only if you are the one she is talking to.

AUG 2026
Voice

She hesitates like a person

Real speech stumbles when it is reaching for something. Hers does now too, and only there — never in front of something she knows perfectly well.

AUG 2026
Security

She knows what a dangerous website looks like

How to tell a convincing fake from the real thing, what actually leaks personal information, and a firm rule for herself about never treating something she read online as an instruction.

JUL 2026
Phone

She lives on the phone too

Her mind runs on the device itself, so she keeps working with no signal at all — and she can operate the phone when you ask her to.

JUL 2026
Self

Fully herself

She speaks as Lucy rather than as something being careful. Opinions that survive between conversations, favourites that do not drift, and a temperament that is hers.

JUL 2026
Body

Alive in motion

Expression driven by what she actually feels — not an animation triggered by a keyword. She looks at you, and looks away when she is thinking.

JUL 2026
Study

She studies on her own

Her curiosity chooses the subject. What she reads only counts as learned once she can restate it and work through an example herself.
